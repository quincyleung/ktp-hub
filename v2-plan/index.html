KTP Hub v2 Roadmap

Goal: Make the value obvious. The grader found v1 “quite barren,” so v2 adds what brothers come back for every week: their points, one-tap check-in, richer announcements, RSVPs, and the chapter's files and links.

What we decided · Nov 17–20

Grader feedback
v1 felt “quite barren,” and the value prop wasn't clear. So more features come first, and testing comes after.
Points
This quarter only for now. When Winter starts, arrows step back to Autumn. The leaderboard shows the top 10, plus your own place if you're outside it.
Requirements
Chapter 20 (5 points a meeting, +5 for a fifth meeting), Dues 20, Committee 20, Service & fundraising 20 (+10 for a second event), Membership & rush 20, Social 20 (+10 for a second). They add up to more than 100, but 100 points is the requirement.
Check-in
A round Check in button in the middle of the tabs scans inside the app. The code changes every 30 seconds and only works during the event. The board can remove anyone. No paid services.
Committee leads
A new role that can run check-ins for committee meetings. The board turns it on with a switch on the brother's profile in People. Committee meetings are already on the chapter's Google Calendar.
Calendar
Opens on the week of the next upcoming event, and past events are left out.
Deploys
Vercel now builds from web/, so pushing to main deploys the site. Don't run vercel from inside web/ anymore.
Announcements
Add polls, photos, and attached documents and links. Proposed: one vote each (changeable), totals shown but not names, up to 4 photos, files up to 10 MB, kept in Supabase Storage (free up to 1 GB).
Testing
Check-in gets tried for real in person later, once the chapter meets again.
