KTP Hub v1 Roadmap

Core task: A brother signs in, adds KTP Hub to their home screen, turns on notifications, and gets a push when the board posts an event or announcement. The post then shows on the homepage.

What we decided · Nov 13–14

Core task
“Don't miss an event.” It's the riskiest technology, so it goes first, and it goes after the main complaint.
Announcements
Added Nov 14. They share the events' post-and-push path: an announcement is a post with a message instead of a time and place. The board decides per post whether to send a push (“Notify everyone,” on by default).
First push
A welcome push the moment someone taps Allow, so a new person finishes the task alone without waiting for the board.
iPhone install
If the app is opened in Safari instead of from the home screen, it shows the Share → Add to Home Screen steps.
Non-members
Guest mode: any email can sign in. Non-roster emails land in a demo chapter with sample events.
Sun Nov 15
Real-user test after the chapter meeting. Attendance stays on the sheet.
Not in v1
Points and attendance, calendar, directory, Partiful links, QR check-in, leaderboard, email backup.
