Design, Build, Ship Project Proposal · Quincy Leung

KTP Hub

KTP actives who are only somewhat involved miss events and don’t know where they stand, because announcements are scattered across email, Messenger and Slack, and points live in a Google Sheet no one checks.

One app for our chapter's announcements, events, and requirement points, with phone notifications and attendance that updates points automatically.

The problem

Fragmented and outdated

3channels for announcements: email, Messenger, and Slack, which few people have on their phones
Week 6and the points tracker is still blank, so nobody knows their standing
By handthe board copies each sign-in sheet (chapter, fundraisers, socials) into the tracker

Users

Who it's for, and what they told me

Actives who are somewhat involved

The members who show up sometimes but not to everything. They don't make the effort to look for communication and need it to be accessible.

The e-board

Posts announcements, takes attendance, and keeps the points tracker. Needs to see what each brother is doing without copying between sheets.

What they told me

  • They saw announcements over email, or heard about events from other brothers and realized they missed it.
  • They skip chapter when they're too busy; seniors often don't care or would rather do something else.
  • They don't know their standing.
  • When they went to something, it was because other brothers were going.
  • To find information, they go on what they know or ask people.

Greek-life apps like OurHouse, Greek Chapter, and CliqWave already bundle announcements, events, and points, yet KTP chapters at Boston, Michigan, and Georgia each built their own. Every chapter's rules are different, and ours aren't served by a generic tool.

Focus

Holistic Journey

Holistic Journey

The value of having an app is the whole flow. Going deep on one piece wouldn't fix the problem.

The journey: the Nov 15 chapter meeting

  1. Board · once
    Imports the roster. Brothers sign up with a roster email, add the app to their home screen, and allow notifications.
  2. Board
    Creates the chapter meeting.
  3. Brother
    Gets a push notification and sees the meeting on the homepage and calendar.
  4. Board · at the meeting
    Opens the roster in the app and taps who's there.
  5. Brother
    Sees their points update right away on the Points tab.

How it's built

Tech stack

PieceChoiceWhy it fits
AppNext.js on VercelPages and the API route that sends notifications live in one codebase, deployed where this page is.
StylingTailwind CSSFast to build mobile-first screens, since brothers will mostly use this on their phones.
Phone install + pushInstallable web app + Web Push (web-push)Push works on iPhone once the app is on the home screen, without the $99/year Apple account a native app needs.
DataSupabase PostgresMembers, events, and attendance in one place, connected to Clerk sign-ins. Points are calculated from attendance, so they're never out of date.
Sign-inClerk (email code)Ready-made sign-in screens. Codes work inside the home-screen app, where a sign-in link would open in Safari instead. Only emails on the roster get in, and board vs. member roles come from the roster.
EventsPartiful link + "Add to Google Calendar"Partiful has no public API, so RSVPs stay where people already use them. No calendar API needed.
RosterOne-time import from our Google SheetThe board's existing list becomes the starting data.

MCP servers

  • Supabase: create tables, run migrations, and check data from Claude Code
  • Google Drive: read the roster and attendance sheets to build the import
  • Claude in Chrome: click through the journey on the live site

CLIs

  • Vercel: deploys and environment variables (like push keys)
  • Supabase: database migrations kept in git
  • GitHub (gh): repo and pull requests

Everything runs on free tiers: Vercel Hobby, Supabase Free, Clerk Free, and Web Push. About 80 members is well under the limits. Supabase pauses free projects after a week without activity, so I'll keep it in use over Thanksgiving.
